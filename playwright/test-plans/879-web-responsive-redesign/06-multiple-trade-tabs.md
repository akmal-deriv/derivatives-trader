# Suite 6 — Multiple Trade Tabs

> **Story:** Support for multiple independent trade tabs so users can monitor and switch between different market/trade setups without losing context.
> **Owner:** @nijil-deriv
> **Result of manual execution (2026-08-03): mixed.** Core tab mechanics (add/switch/remove/duplicate-handling/reload persistence/active-tab-scoped Buy) all work correctly on desktop. However, two real issues were found: **per-tab Stake does not reliably persist** (TABS-03), and **the 4-tab maximum from Figma is not enforced on desktop** (TABS-14), even though mobile does block "Add market" at 4 tabs.

## Confirmed Behavior (exploration + Figma + manual execution)

- Selecting a market from "Add market" adds a new tab (icon + market name + trade type) next to existing ones. The close (×) icon only renders for the active tab by default — **hovering a non-active tab also reveals its own × icon**, so any tab can be closed directly, not just the active one.
- Switching tabs updates the URL's `symbol`/`trade_type` query params and the active tab's border color updates ✅.
- **Mobile**: at 4 tabs, the "Add market" button itself becomes `disabled` — you can't even open the market selection modal for a 5th.
- **Desktop**: at 4 tabs, "Add market" is NOT disabled — clicking it opens the modal, and selecting a market **successfully adds a 5th tab**. A generic red toast — _"Sorry, an error occurred while processing your request."_ — flashed briefly when the modal opened, not the Figma-specified _"You can open up to 4 tabs at a time..."_ message. This looks like a genuine gap/bug: the 4-tab cap from Figma ("Farid - MAX TABS allowed scenario") is enforced on mobile but not desktop.
- **Environmental note**: the Demo account used for this session (`drvtstqa_dtrader@webapps.mailisk.net`) is a shared staging QA account — other open positions (e.g. a 500 USD Multipliers Down on Volatility 100, a 100 USD Rise on Bear Market Index) appeared and resolved independently of this testing session's actions. Position counts and balance shifting on their own are not related to these test cases — attribute a balance/position change to a specific test only when the delta matches an action just taken (as done for TABS-12).

## Preconditions (all cases)

- Desktop ≥1280×800 unless noted; started from a single default tab (Volatility 100 (1s) Index, Rise/Fall)
- Executed on the shared Demo account — see environmental note above

## Notes

1. **TABS-03 and TABS-14 are the two real defects found in this suite.** Both are P0. Recommend filing these against @nijil-deriv before the redesign merges:
    - Stake is not reliably retained per-tab (may reset to default, or transiently show another tab's value).
    - Desktop does not enforce the Figma-specified 4-tab maximum; only mobile does.
2. The hover-to-reveal-× behavior for non-active tabs works correctly and isn't itself a gap — worth confirming it's discoverable enough for real users (no visual affordance shows it's there until hover/tap-and-hold).
